The count_01.wav through count_60.wav assets are adapted human recordings.
Changes: silence/mouth-noise trimming, peak normalization, endpoint fading,
24 kHz mono PCM16 conversion, digit concatenation for 11-60, and
pitch-preserving tempo adjustment for longer numbers.
